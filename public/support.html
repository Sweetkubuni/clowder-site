Support

Clowder is made by Oniru LLC. If something isn't working, you have a question about your account or a purchase, or you want to report something you saw in the app, write to us. A person reads every message.

Email us
justin.barnes@onirudesign.com

We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your Clowder @username and, for a purchase, the date and the token pack you bought.

Report a person, a room or a message

Email us with the @username or room name, what happened and roughly when. We review reports and act on them, which can mean removing content, removing a user from a room, or closing a room.

Purchases and refunds

Token packs are bought through the App Store, so Apple handles payment and refunds. To ask Apple for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. If you were charged but your tokens didn't arrive, email us first: we can usually fix it.

Delete your account or your data

Email us from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. What we delete and what we have to keep is explained in our privacy policy.
