Report abuse or unsafe content

The fastest way is in the app: open the person's profile or press and hold the message, then choose Report. You can also Block someone so they can no longer message you. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break our rules.

You can also email support@onirudesign.com with the room name, the username, and what happened.

Common questions

  • Purchases and refunds. Tokens are bought through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are requested from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play. If tokens didn't arrive after a purchase, email us with the date and the pack you bought.
  • Deleting your account. Delete it in the app, or email us from your account's address and we'll do it for you.
  • Voice calls. If others can't hear you, check that Clowder has microphone access in your device settings.
