Child Safety Standards

Clowder is made by Oniru LLC. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Clowder does not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualised comments about minors, sextortion, or any attempt to contact a minor for sexual purposes. Accounts that break this rule are removed permanently.

Who can use Clowder

Clowder is for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher, to use it (see our Terms of Use). If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we remove it.

What is prohibited

  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material in any form, including drawn or generated images and text.
  • Sexualising minors in messages, usernames, profiles, room names or voice calls.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a minor, or with someone presenting as a minor, in order to abuse or exploit them.
  • Sextortion or threats to share intimate images.
  • Trying to arrange contact with a minor, on or off Clowder, for any sexual purpose.

How to report

In the app, press and hold a message and choose Report message, or open a room or a profile, tap … and choose Report. Pick the reason that fits, such as Underage user, and add details. You can also Block the person at once.

You can also email support@onirudesign.com with the username, the room and what happened. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

What we do

  • We review reports within 24 hours, and reports about children first.
  • We remove content that breaks these standards, take down the room where needed and permanently remove the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, as United States law requires, and to the relevant authorities elsewhere where the law requires it.
  • We preserve the related records as the law requires and cooperate with law enforcement requests.
  • Room hosts can kick and mute people, every user can block anyone, and an automatic filter blocks known abusive words in chat.

Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child safety and CSAM prevention is justin.barnes@onirudesign.com. Law enforcement can use the same address.
