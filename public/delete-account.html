Delete your Clowder account

Clowder is an app by ONIRU LLC. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

The fastest way: in the app

iPhone
  1. Open Clowder and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read what is deleted and kept, enter your password, and tap Delete my account.
Android
  1. Open Clowder and tap ⚙ (Settings) on Home.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read what is deleted and kept, and tap Delete my account. Guests don't need a password.

Do you have tokens left? They are lost when you delete your account and we can't refund them. Purchases stay refundable through the store you bought them from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play through Google Play Help. Ask for a refund before you delete.

If you can't open the app

Email justin.barnes@onirudesign.com with the subject "Delete my Clowder account".

  • If you signed up with an email address (every iPhone account): send the request from that address and include your @username. We delete the account once we've confirmed it's yours.
  • If you used Clowder as a guest (Android): a guest account has no email address, so the in-app steps above are the way to delete it. If you can't open the app any more, email us and we'll explain what we need to identify the account.

What is deleted

Your profile, friends, follows, blocks, scheduled shows and notification settings, and your messages are removed from rooms and direct messages. Live rooms you host are closed. Deletion finishes within 24 hours and can't be undone.

Your username is held for 90 days after deletion, then anyone can claim it.

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase and refund records, for 7 years, for tax, accounting and App Store or Google Play refund disputes.
  • A copy of your messages and room activity in a restricted safety archive. Messages are kept 180 days, or 365 days if they were reported or blocked; room activity is kept 365 days. They are used only for abuse reports and legal requests, then deleted.
  • Reports you filed or that name you, for 365 days after they are resolved.
  • A record that you asked for deletion.
  • Data we're legally required to keep can take longer to delete.

Voice calls are never recorded.
