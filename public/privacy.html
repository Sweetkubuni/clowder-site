Privacy Policy

Clowder, by Oniru LLC · Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Oniru LLC ("we", "us") collects when you use the Clowder app on iOS or Android, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email support@onirudesign.com.

Information you give us

  • Account. You can use Clowder as a guest or sign in with an email address. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile. Your display name, username, bio, avatar, the topics you choose, and any social links you add. Your profile is visible to other Clowder users.
  • Messages and rooms. Messages you send in rooms and direct messages, and the rooms you create or join.
  • Reports. If you report a user or message, we keep the report and any details you write so we can review it.

Information collected automatically

  • Voice calls. Voice calls are relayed in real time through Cloudflare's calling service. We do not record or store call audio. We keep a record of when you joined and left a call.
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, we store your device's notification token (via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send you alerts.
  • Technical data. Our servers log requests (such as time, IP address and the action taken) to keep the service running and secure. We may use crash and error reporting to fix problems; these reports are stripped of sign-in credentials.

Purchases

Token purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details. We receive a record of the purchase so we can add tokens to your account, and we keep a ledger of your token balance and spending.

How we use information

  • To run Clowder: deliver messages, match you to rooms, connect calls, and keep your token balance.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, enforce blocks, and act on content that breaks our rules.
  • To fix bugs and protect the service against abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the providers that run Clowder for us: Google Firebase (sign-in and notifications), Cloudflare (voice calls and network delivery), DigitalOcean (our servers), and Apple and Google (purchases). We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep it

  • Rooms are temporary; a room's live chat ends when the room expires.
  • We keep an archive of messages for up to 180 days, and up to 365 days for messages involved in a report or block, so we can review abuse. Other room records are kept for up to 365 days.
  • Resolved reports are deleted 365 days after they are resolved.
  • Purchase and token records are kept as long as needed for accounting and refunds.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • You can block or report other users from their profile or messages.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or by emailing support@onirudesign.com. Deleting your account removes your profile and sign-in. We may keep limited records where the law requires it or where they are part of an open safety investigation.
  • You can turn off notifications in your device settings.

Children

Clowder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
