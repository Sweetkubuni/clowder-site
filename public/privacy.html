Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026. This policy explains what information Oniru LLC ("we", "us") collects when you use the Clowder app and its servers, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Questions: justin.barnes@onirudesign.com.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication (we never see your password). An internal user ID is created for your account.
  • Profile. Your @username, display name, and anything you add: a bio, an avatar, links, and the topics you pick as interests.
  • What you post. Chat messages you send in rooms and direct messages, and records of room activity such as rooms you create, join or leave, and calls you join.
  • Voice. When you join a voice call, your audio is relayed in real time to the other people in the call through Cloudflare's real-time media service. We do not record calls.
  • Safety. Reports you file (including any description you write) and the people you block.
  • Purchases. When you buy tokens, Apple processes the payment. We receive the App Store transaction ID and product, and we keep a record of your token balance and every token credit and spend. We issue a random purchase ID tied to your account, which the app attaches to App Store purchases so tokens reach the right account. We never receive your card details.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device (through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which also delivers to Apple devices).
  • Technical data. Our servers log each request, including your IP address, the time and the address requested. If the app or server hits an error, an error report (device and app details and the error) may be sent to Sentry. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the app: create your account, show your profile, deliver messages and calls, and match you into rooms by topic.
  • To process purchases and keep your token balance correct, including handling refunds Apple tells us about.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, enforce blocks, remove content or users who break the rules, and prevent abuse such as spam (for example by rate-limiting).
  • To send notifications you've turned on, such as when someone you follow goes live.
  • To find and fix bugs and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising.

Who we share it with

Other Clowder users see your public profile and what you post in rooms you join; direct messages are seen by the people in them. We also use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

Purchase information we may send Apple

When you ask Apple for a refund of a token purchase, Apple may ask us about it. Only if you have agreed to this in the app, we answer with three things: whether the tokens were delivered to your account, how much of the purchase you used, and our refund preference. Apple uses this to decide your refund request. You can withdraw that consent at any time in the app's settings, and we then stop sending it. We keep a record of each consent and withdrawal for 7 years.

How long we keep it

  • Chat messages: 180 days. Messages that were blocked by our filter or reported: 365 days.
  • Records of room activity: 365 days.
  • Reports: until resolved, then 365 days.
  • Server request logs: up to 30 days.
  • Token purchases, credits and spends: kept as financial records, including after an account is deleted.
  • Your account and profile: until you delete your account.

We may keep specific information longer when the law requires it or to deal with a legal claim or safety investigation.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Notifications can be turned off in your device's settings.
  • Delete your account by emailing us from your sign-up address. We delete your sign-in, replace your profile with an anonymous placeholder, and remove your information except the records described above, which expire on their own schedule.
  • Access or correct your information by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to some uses. We will not treat you differently for using them.

Children

Clowder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We limit who can reach our servers and databases, keep our databases off the public internet, and use access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
